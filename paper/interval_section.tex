\subsection{From points to intervals}
\label{sec:interval}

For Conditions~\ref{cond:fp}--\ref{cond:lc} the strip certificates
already give statements on intervals of $\kappa$: each slab is
certified with every input enclosed over the whole slab.
Condition~\ref{cond:2var} is different.  Over a slab every certificate must hold with
$(\alpha,q_0,\psi_0)$ replaced by their hulls over the slab.  The
per-slab $\alpha_\star$ intervals of Section~\ref{sec:slabs} are wider than
$3.4\cdot10^{-3}$, and across such an interval the fixed point moves in
$q_0$ at rate about $0.9$, so the hulls are about $3\cdot10^{-3}$ wide in
$q_0$; the induced enclosure of $a^\star$ has
radius at least a few times $10^{-3}$, and at
that radius the Region~II cell enclosures are about $\pm0.2$, while
the thinnest Region~II cells have slack $5\cdot10^{-4}$ and the
thinnest Region~I bands between $1.1\cdot10^{-5}$ and
$7.5\cdot10^{-5}$.  An interval theorem for Condition~\ref{cond:2var}
therefore needs a certified $\kappa$-derivative of each certificate,
with the fixed-point branch differentiated implicitly, and quadratic
control with explicit remainder bounds on the annulus between the
star and the far cells, where the certified Hessian alone does not
reach.
