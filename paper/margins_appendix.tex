\section{Further margins}\label{sec:margins}

The four conditions of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} are margin-wise
statements, and the certificates of
Sections~\ref{sec:twovar}--\ref{sec:regionI} run unchanged at any
$\kappa$ of the strip: the program locates the $(q_0,\psi_0)$ boxes at both endpoints of the
$\alpha$ interval, configures the exact-arithmetic modules from those
balls, and runs the same four pieces.  Besides $\kappa=0.05$ it
certifies Condition~\ref{cond:2var} at $\kappa=-0.45$, $-0.05$,
$0.0995$ and $0.13$.  At the two negative margins the fixed-point
uniqueness input comes entirely from the contraction certificates of
Section~\ref{sec:slabs}.  Table~\ref{tab:margins} lists the certified
pieces at the five margins.

For each band, the Region-I manifest of every margin carries an
interval matrix $B$ with $\nabla^2\Phi(\lambda)\preceq B$ in the
Loewner order (the difference is positive semidefinite) for every
$\lambda$ in the band's certified dual box, so that the Hessian of
the entropy term is at most $-B^{-1}$ there.  The Lean files check
that every band's $B$ is positive definite, in thirteen groups of
$108$ bands with a checked total count ($55$ theorems per margin; the
mutation battery rejects a sign change in each group of theorems).

The stage-2 containment reads each supplement arc on both angular
branches, so a cell sees every arc whichever branch its interval lies
on.  At $\kappa=0.0995$ the exclusion box has upper offset $+0.1030$.
Just above the smaller offset $+0.0961$ the dual certificate of
Region~II degenerates along a thin ridge: on a cell of width $0.0018$
the certified value interval is $[\pm 0.181]$, and the bound is
negative again two cell-widths higher.  Enlarging the box needs no
change to Region~I, because the stage-2 containment derives its
tiling from the box and fails if the enlarged region leaves what the
star and the supplement cover.
