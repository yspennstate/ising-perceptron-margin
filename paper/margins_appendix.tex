\subsection{Further margins}\label{sec:margins}

Huang's four conditions are statements at one margin, and the certificates of
Sections~\ref{sec:twovar}--\ref{sec:regionI} run unchanged at any
$\kappa$ of the strip, with the boxes of $(q_0,\psi_0)$ located at
both ends of the $\alpha_\star$ interval of that margin.  Besides
$\kappa=0.05$, Condition~\ref{cond:2var} holds at $\kappa=-0.45$, $-0.05$,
$0.0995$ and $0.13$.  At the four margins with $\kappa\le0.10$ the
uniqueness of the fixed point comes from the contraction certificates
of Section~\ref{sec:slabs} alone; at $\kappa=0.13$ Nakajima's
uniqueness theorem gives it as well.  Table~\ref{tab:margins} lists the certified
leaves and bands at the five margins.

For each band, the ray-certificate file of every margin carries
a Loewner majorant $\bar B$, an interval matrix with $\nabla^2\Phi(\lambda)\preceq\bar B$ in the
Loewner order (the difference is positive semidefinite) for every
$\lambda$ in the band's dual enclosure, so that the Hessian of
the entropy term is at most $-\bar B^{-1}$ there.  Every band's $\bar B$ is
positive definite, a fact checked in exact rational arithmetic
(Section~\ref{sec:metaverify}).
