\section{Toward the matching lower bound}\label{sec:lower}

Write $m_N(\kappa)$ for the largest $m$ such that some
$w\in\{-1,+1\}^N$ satisfies $\langle g_a,w\rangle/\sqrt N\ge\kappa$
for all $a\le m$.  Krauth and M\'ezard conjectured the value of the
limiting ratio $m_N(\kappa)/N$ for every margin, not only at
$\kappa=0$~\cite{krauth1989storage}; the constant $0.833$ is the
zero-margin point of the conjectured curve $\alpha_\star(\kappa)$.
At $\kappa=0$ the conjecture is resolved: Ding and
Sun~\cite{ding2019capacity} proved $\alpha_\star$ is a lower bound
with positive probability, the sharp-threshold sequence of Xu~\cite{xu2021} and Nakajima
and Sun~\cite{nakajimasun2022} upgrades positive probability to
convergence in probability, Huang~\cite{huang2024capacity} proved the
matching upper bound, and the numerical hypotheses of both proofs are
certified~\cite{shmalo2026capacity}.  Away from $\kappa=0$ the upper
half is Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.  For the lower half, the
replica-symmetric free energy is proved at small constraint density
by Talagrand~\cite{talagrand2000} and, for a wider class of
activations, by Bolthausen, Nakajima, Sun, and Xu~\cite{bnsx2022};
no lower bound matching $\alpha_\star(\kappa)$ is known at
$\kappa\ne0$.  There are three partial results: a
lemma showing that plain second moments fail at every margin, an
embedded bound that gives capacity lower bounds for $\kappa<0$, and a
floating-point evaluation of the $\kappa$-dependent inputs of the
Ding--Sun argument at the five margins.

\subsection{Plain second moments fail at every margin}

Write $\mathcal Z=\mathcal Z_{N,\kappa}$ for the number of $w\in\{-1,+1\}^N$ satisfying
all $m=\lceil\alpha N\rceil$ constraints, and
$\varpi_\rho(\kappa)=\Prob(X\ge\kappa,\,Y\ge\kappa)$ for $(X,Y)$ bivariate
standard Gaussian with correlation $\rho$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:secondmoment}
For every $\kappa\in\R$ and $\alpha>0$,
\[
\liminf_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log\frac{\E[\mathcal Z^2]}{(\E \mathcal Z)^2}
\;\ge\; \delta(\alpha,\kappa)\;>\;0 .
\]
Consequently the guarantee of the Paley--Zygmund bound,
$\Prob(\mathcal Z>0)\ge(\E \mathcal Z)^2/\E[\mathcal Z^2]$, is at most $e^{-\delta N+o(N)}$, and no
bound depending on $\E \mathcal Z$ and
$\E[\mathcal Z^2]$ alone can certify more, since a distribution placing mass
$(\E \mathcal Z)^2/\E[\mathcal Z^2]$ at $\E[\mathcal Z^2]/\E \mathcal Z$ and the rest at zero matches
both moments.  For $\kappa\ge0$ the same conclusion holds for every
weighted count $\mathcal Z_f=\sum_w\prod_a f(\langle g_a,w\rangle/\sqrt N)$
with $f\ge0$ supported on $[\kappa,\infty)$ and
$0<\int f\varphi\,dx<\infty$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$\E \mathcal Z=2^N\Psi(\kappa)^m$ by independence over constraints.  For a
pair $w,w'$ at overlap $\rho$ the constraint fields are bivariate
Gaussian with correlation $\rho$, so counting ordered pairs by
Hamming distance $k$,
\[
\E[\mathcal Z^2]=2^N\sum_{k=0}^N\binom Nk\,\varpi_{1-2k/N}(\kappa)^m .
\]
Fix $\rho_0\in(0,1)$, let $k^*=\lceil N(1-\rho_0)/2\rceil$ and
$\rho^*=1-2k^*/N$.  Keeping the single term $k^*$ and using
$\binom Nk\ge e^{N h(k/N)}/(N+1)$ with $h$ the natural-log binary
entropy ($h(p)=h(1-p)$, so the exponent may be written with
$(1+\rho_0)/2$),
\[
\frac1N\log\frac{\E[\mathcal Z^2]}{(\E \mathcal Z)^2}
\ge\underbrace{h\Bigl(\tfrac{1+\rho_0}2\Bigr)-\log2
+\alpha\bigl[\log \varpi_{\rho_0}(\kappa)-2\log\Psi(\kappa)\bigr]}
_{=:\,\zeta_{\alpha,\kappa}(\rho_0)}\;+\;O(N^{-1}\log N),
\]
the error, of order $N^{-1}\log N$, collecting the binomial
prefactor, the $|\rho^*-\rho_0|\le
2/N$ continuity of $\rho\mapsto\log \varpi_\rho$ (differentiable, below),
and $|m-\alpha N|\le1$.  Now $\varpi_0=\Psi(\kappa)^2$ and $h(1/2)=\log2$
give $\zeta_{\alpha,\kappa}(0)=0$.  Reflecting the orthant to a joint
CDF, $\varpi_\rho(\kappa)=N_2(-\kappa,-\kappa;\rho)$ with $N_2$ the
bivariate normal distribution function, and Plackett's
identity $\partial N_2/\partial\rho=\varphi_2$ with the symmetry
$\varphi_2(-\kappa,-\kappa;\rho)=\varphi_2(\kappa,\kappa;\rho)$ give
$\partial \varpi_\rho/\partial\rho=\varphi_2(\kappa,\kappa;\rho)$ on
$(-1,1)$; at $\rho=0$ this is $\varphi(\kappa)^2$.  Since
$\frac{d}{d\rho}h(\tfrac{1+\rho}2)=\tfrac12\log\tfrac{1-\rho}{1+\rho}$
vanishes at $0$,
\[
\zeta_{\alpha,\kappa}'(0)=\alpha\,
\frac{\varphi(\kappa)^2}{\Psi(\kappa)^2}\;>\;0 ,
\]
so $\zeta_{\alpha,\kappa}(\rho_0)\ge\rho_0\,\zeta_{\alpha,\kappa}'(0)/2>0$ for all
small $\rho_0>0$; fix such a $\rho_0$ and set
$\delta=\zeta_{\alpha,\kappa}(\rho_0)$.  For the weighted counts, the diagonal
term of $\E[\mathcal Z_f^2]$ is $2^N\E[f(X)^2]^m$, so either
$\E[\mathcal Z_f^2]=\infty$ and the claim is trivial, or $f\in L^2(\varphi\,dx)$ and
Mehler's expansion gives
$\E[f(X)f(Y)]=\sum_{j\ge0}\rho^j\langle f,\mathrm{He}_j\rangle^2/j!$,
whose $\rho$-derivative at $0$ is $(\int xf\varphi\,dx)^2$; for
$f\ge0$ supported on $[\kappa,\infty)$ with $\kappa\ge0$ the
integrand $xf(x)\varphi(x)$ is nonnegative, and it vanishes almost
everywhere only if $f$ vanishes almost everywhere off the origin,
which $0<\int f\varphi\,dx$ excludes; the argument
then runs unchanged.
\end{proof}

The mechanism is the asymmetry: pairs of solutions at small positive
overlap are exponentially favored over independent pairs at every
margin, which is what the AMP-conditioned second moment
of~\cite{ding2019capacity} is built to circumvent at $\kappa=0$, and
what any lower-bound proof must circumvent at every other margin.
At $\kappa=0$ the failure is classical (see the introduction
of~\cite{ding2019capacity}); Lemma~\ref{lem:secondmoment} extends it
to every $\kappa$ and, for $\kappa\ge0$, to every weighted count.  For $\kappa<0$ the argument gives nothing for some weighted counts: a
weight symmetric about the origin, such as the indicator
$f=\mathbf 1_{[-|\kappa|,|\kappa|]}$ of an interval, has
$\int xf\varphi\,dx=0$, so the first-order term vanishes.

\subsection{A capacity lower bound at negative margins}

\begin{prop}\label{prop:embedded}
Let $\kappa<0$ and let $\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(r)=
\log2/(-\log\Prob(|X|\le r))$ denote the symmetric binary perceptron
threshold at width $r$.  For every
$\alpha<\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(|\kappa|)$, with probability
$1-o(1)$ the margin-$\kappa$ perceptron admits a solution, that is,
$m_N(\kappa)/N\ge\alpha$.
\end{prop}

\begin{proof}
If $|\langle g_a,w\rangle|/\sqrt N\le-\kappa$ for all $a$ then in
particular $\langle g_a,w\rangle/\sqrt N\ge\kappa$, so the symmetric
solution set at width $|\kappa|$ embeds in the margin-$\kappa$
solution set.  The value $\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(r)$ is the
first-moment threshold of Aubin, Perkins and
Zdeborov\'a~\cite{aubin2019storage}.  Altschuler's
Theorem~1.4~\cite{altschuler2022} states that for every width $r>0$
and every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $c>0$ such that, when the rows
$g_a/\sqrt N$ are independent $N(0,N^{-1}I_N)$ vectors and their
number is below $\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(r)N-c\log N$, a binary $w$
with $|\langle g_a,w\rangle|/\sqrt N\le r$ for every $a$ exists with
probability at least $1-\varepsilon$ for $N$ large.  Its proof uses the second-moment
condition of Abbe, Li and Sly~\cite[Lemma~3.4]{abbelisly2021} (the
second-moment exponent is largest at overlap zero), which they prove
for every width in their Section~4.7.1.  At fixed
$\alpha<\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(|\kappa|)$ the row count $\alpha N$ is
eventually below $\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(|\kappa|)N-c\log N$, so a
symmetric solution of width $|\kappa|$ exists with probability
$1-o(1)$.  (The lognormal limit of Abbe, Li,
and Sly~\cite{abbelisly2021} is proved for Rademacher disorder, and
Perkins and Xu~\cite{perkinsxu2021} treat the Gaussian case under a
single-critical-point hypothesis, so neither alone covers the model
here.)
\end{proof}

The bound should be read next to monotonicity.  A solution at
margin $0$ is a solution at every $\kappa<0$, so
$m_N(\kappa)\ge m_N(0)$, and at $\kappa=0$ the ratio $m_N(0)/N$
converges in probability to $\alpha_\star(0)\approx0.8330786$ (the
lower half by~\cite{ding2019capacity} with the sharp-threshold
transfer of~\cite{nakajimasun2022}, its numerical hypotheses
certified in~\cite{shmalo2026capacity}).  Hence
for $\kappa<0$
\[
\frac{m_N(\kappa)}{N}\;\ge\;
\max\bigl\{\alpha_\star(0),\,\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(|\kappa|)\bigr\}
-o(1)\quad\text{whp}.
\]
At the two certified negative margins monotonicity is the stronger
input: the embedding gives $0.215$ at $\kappa=-0.05$ and $0.655$ at
$\kappa=-0.45$, against the predicted $0.889$ and $1.558$.  The
embedding takes over only for $\kappa<-0.5757$, where
$\alpha^{\mathrm{sym}}_c(|\kappa|)$ passes $\alpha_\star(0)$.  Li,
Schramm, and Zhou~\cite{lsz2024} prove lower bounds on the
satisfiability threshold of the same one-sided perceptron at
negative margin by a different route, with the sharpest statements
in the $\kappa\to-\infty$ regime.

\subsection{The Ding--Sun condition at general margin}

The construction underlying the $\kappa=0$ lower bound
of~\cite{ding2019capacity} is written at general margin throughout
their Section~2: their equations (34)--(48) define, for parameters
$(\kappa_{\mathrm{all}},\alpha_{\mathrm{all}})$, an explicit
univariate function $S_{\kappa,\alpha}(\omega)$ on
$[\omega_{\min},1]$ built from the fixed point $(q_\star,\psi_\star)$
(an entropy term through the two-spin measure $P_{H,D}$, a
constraint term through the conditional Mills law
$\varphi_\xi$, and a convex $\inf_s$ correction), and their
Condition~1.2 is the statement $S_{0,\alpha_\star}(\omega)<0$ for
$\omega\notin\{0,1\}$.  The $\kappa$-analog of that hypothesis is
therefore already defined in their own notation, and its
$\kappa$-dependent inputs are the fixed point, its uniqueness
rectangle and the crossing, which are given by
Theorem~\ref{thm:strip} and the enclosures of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

In floating point, with Gauss--Hermite and conditional
Gauss--Legendre quadrature, the function
$S_{\kappa,\alpha_\star(\kappa)}$ behaves uniformly at the five
certified margins and at $\kappa=0$
(Figure~\ref{fig:dscondition}): at every margin the curve is
strictly negative off $\{0,1\}$, at most
$-5\cdot10^{-5}$ outside windows of half-width $0.02$ around the two
roots (inside them it climbs to its maxima at the roots, reaching
$-1.6\cdot10^{-6}$ at the closest grid points), the maximum at
$\omega=0$ is degenerate exactly as
at $\kappa=0$, and the whole family collapses nearly onto a single
shape, with $\omega_{\min}$ drifting from $-0.441$ at
$\kappa=-0.45$ to $-0.421$ at $\kappa=0.13$.  The inequality
$S_{\kappa,\alpha_\star(\kappa)}(\omega)<0$ for $\omega\notin\{0,1\}$
is not proved at any $\kappa\ne0$.

What separates these verified inputs from a general-margin lower
bound is the probabilistic body of~\cite{ding2019capacity}: the
AMP-conditioned moment computation of their Sections~2--7, whose
displays carry $\kappa_{\mathrm{all}}$ explicitly; by their
Remark~1.5 the assumption $\kappa=0$ enters only in a few steps,
which they indicate.  Extending that body to $\kappa\ne0$ requires, beyond
the certified inputs above: a priori bounds at margin (the upper one
is free at every $\kappa$ from the first moment, since
$\E \mathcal Z\to0$ exponentially above the annealed threshold
$-\log2/\log\Psi(\kappa)$; for $\kappa<0$ the lower one is the
monotonicity bound above), the state-evolution inputs (generic in the
nonlinearity, hence in $\kappa$), and the sharp-threshold transfer
of~\cite{nakajimasun2022} if convergence in probability rather than
positive probability is wanted.
